AI_STATE_IDLEIdle, no target, scanning at the slow idle heartbeat.
IDLE_ACTIVITY_NONENamed sub-states of AI_STATE_IDLE - "Idle" alone used to cover wandering, building, following, hiding, and patrolling all as one flat label with no memory between ticks. See patrol()/xeno_ai_controller.dm's idle_activity var.
AI_STATE_APPROACHINGTarget acquired, closing distance.
AI_STATE_ATTACKINGAdjacent to target, actively attacking.
AI_STATE_RETURNINGDisengaging, walking back to anchor_turf.
AI_STATE_SEARCHINGChasing down the last known position of a target that escaped rather than died (may involve a ladder to cross z-levels).
AI_XENO_DEFAULT_ATTACK_DISTANCEDefault scan radius (tiles) used for both target acquisition and re-acquisition.
AI_XENO_DEFAULT_RETURN_DISTANCEDefault leash radius (tiles) from anchor_turf before an AI xeno disengages and returns.
AI_XENO_TARGET_SCAN_REFRESH_DISTANCEHow far the pilot can drift from where process_target()'s scan rectangle was last centered before it's considered stale and recomputed - keeps the scan following her as she patrols/wanders instead of staying pinned to wherever she happened to be during her last unsuccessful scan.
XENO_MELEE_ATTACK_DELAYPer-swing cooldown the AI's plain melee is held to (execute_attack()'s plain-melee branch, xeno_ai_attack.dm) - attack_alien() itself never sets this, only click_adjacent()'s own next_move += 4 does, and only for a real player's click. 4 is the bare code-permitted floor though (2.5 attacks/sec sustained), far faster than a real player's actual click cadence in practice (aiming, reacting, missing a click here and there) - "no click delay... can melt a player to death with just slashing" was this AI attacking at that literal floor nonstop, which no human sustains. Set higher to read as a real melee pace instead of the theoretical maximum.
AI_XENO_DISARM_CHANCEPercent chance a plain melee swing against a human uses INTENT_DISARM (a real tackle attempt, can knock down) instead of INTENT_HARM (plain claw damage).
AI_XENO_DEFAULT_HEARTBEATTicks slept between ai_loop() iterations while actively engaged with a target. Deliberately as low as BYOND's sleep() grants (1 tick) - actual movement/attack pacing is already gated for free by the pilot's own next_move (same movement_delay()/attack cooldown a real player is bound by via step()/attack_alien()), so there's no reason to lay a second, slower artificial cadence on top of it. At 5 this was the real bottleneck behind "pathfinding far too slow" and "never walks and acts in the same window" - a fast caste's true move_delay could be well under 5 ticks, but tick() (and therefore any new step or attack) couldn't run again until the full 5 had passed regardless.
AI_XENO_DEFAULT_IDLE_HEARTBEATTicks slept between ai_loop() iterations while idle/no target (cheaper than the engaged heartbeat).
AI_XENO_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction (0-1) below which an AI xeno even considers disengaging - see should_flee() for the rest of the decision (target's own health, nearby backup). Raised from 0.25 - "normal casts should really use fleeing better" - combined with the old ally threshold of 1, most fights had an excuse not to disengage until nearly dead.
AI_QUEEN_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTQueen-specific flee threshold, higher than the population default - "she is big and slow and easy to kill," a huge investment worth breaking off with sooner rather than overcommitting.
AI_XENO_FLEE_ALLY_RADIUSTiles within which a living same-hive AI xeno counts as "backup" for should_flee()'s decision.
AI_XENO_FLEE_ALLY_THRESHOLDNearby same-hive allies (see count_nearby_hive_allies()) at/above which a hurt xeno keeps fighting instead of disengaging - not fighting alone, so the hive's numbers carry it instead. Raised from 1 - a single other daughter happening to be nearby isn't real backup, it was just cancelling the flee decision almost every time.
AI_XENO_DESPERATE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction (0-1) below which a fleeing xeno gives up on running and turns to fight instead - "unless they are very desperate and want to live." Well below the flee threshold: by this point a parting hit while her back's turned is just as likely to kill her as standing and swinging, so running only pays off if she can actually reach safety, not just because she's hurt. Only relevant while already fleeing (return_to_anchor()) and only when there's still a real target adjacent to turn on.
AI_DRONE_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTDrone-specific flee threshold, higher than the population default - "many casts like... drones... dive to their deaths." She's a builder pressed into a fight, not a frontline unit, so she should break off sooner than a caste actually built to brawl.
AI_XENO_DEFENSIBLE_SEARCH_RADIUSRadius (tiles) find_defensible_turf() searches for a corner/wall position with limited approach angles - "they should also consider corners and walls as defensive positions" for fleeing/kiting castes instead of always retreating across open ground.
AI_XENO_DEFENSIBLE_MIN_DENSE_SIDESCardinally-adjacent dense tiles a candidate turf needs before find_defensible_turf() considers it "defensible" (fewer open approach directions).
AI_XENO_SEARCH_TIMEOUTHow long an AI xeno will keep investigating a lost target's last known position before giving up.
XENO_PATHFIND_MAX_CELLSLargest local grid (width*height tiles) handed to the native pathfinder. Beyond this the old greedy step_towards() chase handles it fine - this is for routing around nearby obstacles, not long-range travel.
PATH_GOAL_REPLAN_TOLERANCETiles a live target can drift from where a cached path was computed for before advance_along_path() throws it out and replans - keeps a moving target from forcing a fresh plan (and a fresh solver tie-break near corners) every single tick.
AI_XENO_SKIRT_DURATIONHow long a committed obstacle-skirt (navigate_around()/attempt_skirt_obstacle()) keeps walking one direction before it's willing to re-aim at the goal - "long pathfinding to go around a building is not possible" needed enough distance in one direction to actually clear a building's corner, not just one sidestep.
AI_XENO_PATROL_RADIUSHow far an idle xeno will wander from its anchor while patrolling.
AI_XENO_OPPORTUNISTIC_REST_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that a hurt xeno standing on healing-eligible weeds opportunistically rests to heal up, rather than only ever resting once critically hurt and already back at anchor_turf.
AI_XENO_MAX_REST_DURATIONHard cap on how long wander() lets her rest before standing back up regardless of health - see rest_timeout.
AI_XENO_PATROL_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that a patrolling xeno takes a wander step, so it doesn't move every single idle tick.
AI_XENO_WANDER_COMMIT_TIMEHow long wander() commits to a single heading before picking a new one - see wander()'s doc comment for why this exists.
AI_XENO_LONG_PATROL_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick (once no higher-priority order applies) that a xeno starts a long patrol leg instead of short-range wander() - see start_long_patrol().
AI_XENO_LONG_PATROL_LZ_CHANCEPercent chance a long patrol leg specifically targets the marine LZ (once one's been chosen) instead of a random distant point.
AI_XENO_LONG_PATROL_RADIUS_GROWTH_PER_MINUTEHow many tiles current_patrol_radius() grows per minute of round time.
AI_XENO_LONG_PATROL_RADIUS_MAXCap on how far current_patrol_radius() can grow.
AI_DRONE_BUILD_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that an AI Drone attempts a build action (plant weeds) instead of wandering. Kept low - every Drone in the hive rolls this independently on its own idle heartbeat, so even a modest chance adds up fast across a full population ("weeding everywhere").
AI_DEFENSE_BUILD_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that an eligible builder caste (Drone/Hivelord/Burrower) attempts a defensive resin wall at the hive perimeter instead of just weeding - see attempt_build_defense().
AI_XENO_DEFENSE_PERIMETER_MIN_RADIUSNearest a defensive perimeter wall is allowed to anchor_turf - keeps walls from boxing in the hive core itself.
AI_XENO_DEFENSE_PERIMETER_MAX_RADIUSFarthest a defensive perimeter wall is allowed from anchor_turf.
AI_BURROWER_AMBUSH_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that a Burrower actually burrows to ambush instead of wandering - kept low, same reasoning as AI_VENT_AMBUSH_CHANCE.
AI_BURROWER_AMBUSH_ENGAGE_CHANCEPercent chance per movement tick, while chasing a target still out of melee range, that a Burrower burrows in place to set up a tunnel-ambush instead of just walking the rest of the way over.
AI_BURROWER_CAUTIOUS_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction below which a Burrower starts actually weighing whether to retreat mid-fight, once the target's back up and still a real threat - "they should consider slashing until the enemy is up, and then they get to decide to flee or keep fighting," not a flat dice roll after every swing regardless of how the fight's going.
AI_BURROWER_RETREAT_DURATIONHow long a Burrower's tactical retreat lasts once triggered - long enough to actually put distance on the target before reconsidering.
AI_BURROWER_RETREAT_SAFE_DISTANCEDistance from the target a retreating Burrower wants before she ducks back underground to wait out the rest of the retreat safely, rather than just standing in the open.
AI_QUEEN_BUILD_CHANCEPercent chance per idle tick that the Queen attempts a build action - she's a single unit, not a population, so this can run a bit higher than a Drone's without the same compounding effect.
AI_XENO_RANGED_PREFERRED_DISTANCETiles an AI ranged xeno tries to stay from its target - closer than this and it backs off instead of closing to melee.
AI_XENO_RANGED_MIN_DISTANCEIf the target closes to within this distance, a ranged xeno actively backs away instead of just holding position.
AI_XENO_HIVE_ALERT_WINDOWHow long other hive members will respond to the Queen's last hive-alert broadcast before it goes stale (see hive_status.dm's queen_alert_turf).
AI_XENO_HIVE_ALERT_RESPONSE_RANGEHow far away a hive-alert will actually pull an idle builder off what she's doing - a fight on the far side of the map shouldn't empty out every drone's weeding queue.
AI_XENO_PHEROMONE_COSTPlasma cost for an AI xeno's own opportunistic combat pheromone burst - matches emit_pheromones()'s own default cost (general_powers.dm) rather than inventing a separate balance number.
AI_QUEEN_ATTACK_DISTANCEThere's only ever one Queen at a time, so she can afford a much wider awareness radius than population-scale castes.
AI_KING_ATTACK_DISTANCEKing is the same kind of solo boss unit as Queen (one per round, not a population-budgeted caste) - "should be as deadly if not more deadly than the Queen," but had no override at all and was stuck on the flat population-default radius, noticing threats far later and giving up chases far sooner than she does.
AI_QUEEN_ESCORT_RADIUSRadius around the Queen used to count how many same-hive daughters already count as her escort.
AI_QUEEN_ESCORT_MAXStops calling for more escorts once this many are already nearby - "up to 10 daughters."
AI_QUEEN_ESCORT_HOLD_DISTANCETiles from the Queen an escorting daughter holds at instead of stacking directly on her tile.
AI_QUEEN_SCOUT_INTERVALHow often the Queen issues a fresh scouting order while idle.
AI_QUEEN_SCOUT_ORDER_WINDOWHow long other hive members will respond to the Queen's last scouting order before it goes stale.
AI_QUEEN_SCOUT_RADIUS_MINStarting half-width of the Queen's scouting search area, in tiles.
AI_QUEEN_SCOUT_RADIUS_GROWTHHow much the scouting search radius grows each time a new order is issued.
AI_QUEEN_SCOUT_RADIUS_MAXCap on how far the scouting search radius can expand to.
AI_QUEEN_MIN_INITIAL_BUILDSSuccessful plant_weeds actions the Queen commits to before considering her build duties satisfied - "build her hive" as a real, ongoing priority.
AI_QUEEN_LZ_SIEGE_RADIUSRadius around the marine LZ used to judge whether the hive's attack on it counts as a "heavy" siege worth the Queen personally joining.
AI_QUEEN_LZ_SIEGE_THRESHOLDNumber of same-hive AI xenos actively fighting near the LZ before the Queen considers it a heavy siege and moves to join.
AI_QUEEN_REMOUNT_COOLDOWNMinimum time the Queen waits after dismounting before she's willing to mount the ovipositor again - dismounting itself is always instant/unthrottled (a threat is a reflex), only settling back down is a considered decision worth a cooldown so a borderline threat-check doesn't thrash her in and out of ovi every tick.
AI_QUEEN_EXPAND_WEEDS_INTERVALHow often a mounted Queen uses expand_weeds to grow the hive's territory from her throne - "build... in weeded areas" while staying productive on ovi instead of only ever laying eggs.
AI_QUEEN_EXPAND_WEEDS_RADIUSRadius expand_weeds' frontier-tile search covers from the Queen's own position.
AI_QUEEN_GROUP_SCREECH_RADIUSLiving hostiles clustered within this radius of a target before the Queen leads with Screech instead of just closing to melee/spitting range - "Screech is her most powerful ability," worth opening with against a group instead of only using it reactively once already swinging.
AI_QUEEN_GROUP_SCREECH_THRESHOLDMinimum clustered hostiles (the primary target plus this many more) before a pre-emptive Screech opener is worth it - a single marine doesn't justify announcing her position before she's even in range.
AI_CRUSHER_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction below which a Crusher disengages - lower than the population-wide default since it's meant to be the hive's tank, not a caste that breaks off early, but not so low she has no margin left to actually reach safety - "crusher... dive to their deaths."
AI_RAVAGER_REPOSITION_CHANCEPercent chance after a Ravager's attack that it sidesteps to a flanking tile instead of standing still - the behavioral stand-in for its "nimble/evasive" identity (there's no actual dodge/evasion stat on the caste).
AI_RAVAGER_SCISSOR_MIN_TARGETSMinimum nearby hostiles before a Ravager uses Scissor Cut instead of a plain melee swing.
AI_RAVAGER_EMPOWER_MIN_TARGETSMinimum nearby hostiles before a Ravager arms Empower - matches the ability's own super-empower payoff threshold, so it's only used in a real group fight where the buff is worth it.
AI_PRAETORIAN_RETREAT_DURATIONHow long a Praetorian/Ravager's tactical retreat lasts once triggered (see start_tactical_retreat()) - "hop into action, slash marines, and hop back into safety, and repeat."
AI_PRAETORIAN_RETREAT_CHANCEPercent chance per plain melee swing (Acid Ball not currently up) that a Praetorian decides she's landed enough hits and retreats anyway.
AI_RAVAGER_RETREAT_DURATIONSame shape as AI_PRAETORIAN_RETREAT_DURATION - Ravager is meant to behave the same way once she's landed a hit.
AI_RAVAGER_RETREAT_CHANCEPercent chance per plain melee swing (Scissor Cut not currently up) that a Ravager retreats anyway, same reasoning as AI_PRAETORIAN_RETREAT_CHANCE.
AI_LURKER_RETREAT_DURATIONHow long a Lurker's tactical retreat lasts once triggered - "make retreating a strategic part of their attack plan too, as they attack and die."
AI_LURKER_RETREAT_CHANCEPercent chance per plain melee swing (Assassinate not currently up) that a Lurker retreats to re-cloak instead of continuing to trade blows in the open.
AI_RUNNER_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction below which a Runner flees - higher than the population default since she's the hive's glass cannon (lowest HP, no armor), meant to hit and run rather than trade hits.
AI_DEFENDER_SWEEP_MIN_TARGETSMinimum valid targets already adjacent before a Defender uses Tail Sweep instead of a plain melee swing.
AI_BOILER_ATTACK_DISTANCEBoiler's own scan/leash radius - wider than the population default so she notices and starts bombarding from well outside melee range instead of needing a target to wander close first.
AI_BOILER_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction below which a Boiler flees - higher than the population default since she's fire-vulnerable and low on melee damage, and getting run down is close to a worst case for this caste.
AI_BOILER_HIDE_DISTANCEDistance a Boiler retreats to while both her ranged abilities are on cooldown ("attack ranged, hide, then come out again once recharged") - wider than the plain ranged kiting band so she's actually out of easy melee reach while waiting, not just standing at the edge of it.

AI_CARRIER_FLEE_HEALTH_PERCENTHealth fraction below which a Carrier disengages - higher than the population default since she has no offensive tools to actually win a fight she's already losing.

Only rolled once hive-alert/escort/long-patrol-continuation have all already passed (see tick()'s idle chain), so the real-world frequency is well below this number by itself - raised from 5 since that compounded rarity read as "never happens" in practice.

AI_PACK_COHESION_HOLD_DISTANCEHow close counts as "already sticking together" for pack cohesion - stops short of stacking exactly on the buddy's tile.
