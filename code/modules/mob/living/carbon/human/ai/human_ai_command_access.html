/proc/human_ai_job_of_titleThe job with the title, null when there is none
/proc/human_ai_job_ofThe job of a human, null when it has none or is not one that is known
/proc/human_ai_command_jobWhether the job is a whitelisted commander role, the Commanding Officer or what is the equivalent in another faction
/proc/human_ai_command_factionThe faction whose AI humans the player may command from the panel, null when they may command none. Only a living player who holds a commander whitelisted job and the whitelist itself counts, and what they command is their own faction and nobody else's.
