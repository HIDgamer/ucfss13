/proc/attach_human_aiPuts a brain in charge of a clientless human, false if it is not eligible or the population cap is met
/proc/human_ai_attach_refusalWhy a human cannot be given a brain, null when it can
/proc/assign_human_aiHands a human that already exists a brain, which is what the assign tool does for whoever it is pointed at. The faction, when one is given, is set first because it decides the rules of engagement the brain starts with, and the role, when one is given, brings its kit along with it. Returns why it was refused, null when the human has a brain now.
/proc/detach_human_aiTakes the brain off a human, the body itself is left as it is
/proc/reattach_human_ai_on_disconnectPuts a brain back after the player who was piloting an AI spawned body leaves it
