/proc/human_ai_turf_lightWhat a unit can make out. Its eyes are no better than a person's: past the reach of its night vision a thing standing in the dark is hard to make out and then not at all, a dim place takes longer, and a body that is cloaked or camouflaged is only made out from close by. What it wears over its eyes counts, a welding mask pulled down leaves it seeing little more than what is at arm's length. Being shot at from somewhere it cannot make out tells it which way the shot came from and not who fired it or where exactly. How much light there is on the tile: what the lamps give it and what the area gives all of it, the sun or a place that is lit up as a whole, which the lamp count leaves out
