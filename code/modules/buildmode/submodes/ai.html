code/modules/buildmode/submodes/ai.dm

/proc/npc_ai_kindWhat kind of AI a mob can be given: a human, a zombie or a xenomorph, null for a mob that has none to give
/proc/npc_ai_giveGives the mob the AI of its kind. Returns why it was refused, null when it has an AI now. The settings are for humans only, the others have one nature
/proc/npc_ai_takeTakes the AI off the mob and leaves it standing where it is. Returns whether it had one
/datum/buildmode_mode/aiThe AI tool of build mode: it gives an AI to the mobs that are clicked, whatever kind they are as long as the kind has an AI, and takes it off again. It is what the assign tool of the NPC command panel was, humans and also the zombies and the xenomorphs.
