/datum/unit_test/human_ai_friendly_toleranceA friend's stray shots are warned about and forgiven, one who keeps shooting is fought for a while and then forgiven too
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_backup_callThe first contact of a fight is a call for backup naming the area, over the radio with a headset, and the nearest free allies come to it
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_lootingA unit short of a gun, ammunition, medical supplies or tools picks them up off the floor in sight, and leaves the rest alone
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_voice_commandsA friendly player's words near a unit are turned into orders, strangers and the other side are ignored, a name picks who is meant
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_defibrillationA medic with a defibrillator brings a dead friend back when the body would take a shock, mends it first when it would not, and leaves it when it cannot
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_rescueA soldier who is no medic drags a downed friend out of the line of fire, when it is loyal enough
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_window_sightA unit sees what is behind a window, a pane of glass does not hide an enemy from it
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_captivesA soldier with restraints takes a downed enemy prisoner, believes an unarmed enemy who calls out that they give up, and takes it back if they pick up a gun
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_camouflageA sniper in a ghillie suit who has been told to hold rigs the suit up and lies in wait
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_real_loadoutsUnits armed with the loadouts the game hands out draw their gun, and reload it when it runs dry
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_real_medicsUnits armed as medics are medics, treat their own wounds and other people's, and bring the dead back
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_laddersA goal on another level is reached by walking to the ladder that leads there and climbing it, and given up on when no ladder does
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_hold_areaUnits told to hold an area spread over it, take posts of their own and never leave it, whatever they fight
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_calloutsA call-out says where the enemy is by the solid things next to it
/area/human_ai_test_cellsA cell for the tests that take prisoners to one
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_prisoner_escortAn officer who cuffs a man takes him away to the cells and lets go of him there
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_classesClasses fight in their own way: the rusher closes on the enemy where a rifleman stands off, the stealth unit waits until the enemy is close, the suicider arms a grenade and the flanker is picked for the side route
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_forced_entryAn insurgent with a charge blows the bolted door it cannot hack and goes through, a marine with the same charge leaves the door alone
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_hammerA unit with a breaching hammer takes a wall down with it when told to demolish it
/datum/unit_test/human_ai_syntheticsA synthetic is calm and collected unless told otherwise, and mends its dents with a welder and its burns with cable and never with bandages
