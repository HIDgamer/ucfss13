/datum/unit_test/human_ai_garbageWhat the AI owns must be freed when it is let go of. A brain, the squad it was put in, the NPC command console that selected its body and that console's selection are noted by reference, then given what create_and_destroy gives the garbage collector (10 seconds, and nothing is hard deleted), and looked for again: anything still there was held by something. The bodies are not deleted: a human with equipment and a hud is not freed in this game with or without an AI, which would only fill the log with reference searches. Brains are let go of the ways the game does it: taken off a live body, left on one that was killed, and released from the NPC command panel.
