Working Joe
/datum/human_ai_role/working_joe

The Working Joe: a synthetic whose job is keeping the ship clean and in repair. It goes round mending lights, scrubbing mess, welding walls, laying tiles and putting APCs right, comes out with its voice lines now and then, and warns people who do something it does not like. It does not take a swing at anyone for a push or a stray shot, only when it has been bothered more than it will put up with, and then it fights back until it is left alone. The lines are the ones the Joe's own voice synthesizer has, in the sounds it has.
