/datum/npc_command_selection

The AI humans one admin session has selected in the NPC command panel

Vars

faction_scopeThe one faction the panel may command, null for every faction. Everything the panel lists, selects or orders goes through valid(), so this holds it all

Procs

forgetTakes the human out of the selection and stops listening for it being deleted
get_selectedThe only way to read the selection, anything that stopped being commandable is dropped first
on_member_deletedA selected human that is deleted is let go at once, so that a selection nobody asks about again cannot keep it from being freed
validWhether the human is something the panel may command: alive, clientless, piloted by a running brain and of the faction the panel is held to
