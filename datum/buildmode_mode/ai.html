/datum/buildmode_mode/ai

The AI tool of build mode: it gives an AI to the mobs that are clicked, whatever kind they are as long as the kind has an AI, and takes it off again. It is what the assign tool of the NPC command panel was, humans and also the zombies and the xenomorphs.

Vars

factionNull keeps theirs
reachTiles around the click that are given the AI too, 0 is only the mob that was clicked
roleNull is the kit of their job

Procs

giveGives the AI to what was clicked, returns the mobs that took it
takeTakes the AI off what was clicked, returns the mobs that had one
targetsThe mobs a click is about: the mob that was clicked, or the one standing on the tile, or every mob close to it when the mode has a reach
