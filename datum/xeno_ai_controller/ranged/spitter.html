/datum/xeno_ai_controller/ranged/spitter

Spitter - "doesn't have an AI fight preset, they just death charge" was a real bug, not a design gap: this queried /xeno_spit/spitter, a cosmetic subtype Spitter.dm's own base_actions never actually grants (it grants the plain /xeno_spit base type instead) - get_ability()'s locate() only matches a held instance that IS the queried type or a stricter subtype of it, never the other way around, so this always returned null and every Spitter fell straight into ranged.dm's melee fallback, permanently. Fixed to query the type actually granted. That ability still has a genuine 60s cooldown though (general_abilities.dm), so on its own she'd still spend most of a fight in the melee fallback - falls back to Spray Acid (8s cooldown, a real ranged line attack) instead of closing to melee whenever the big spit isn't up, so she stays a ranged threat continuously rather than only once a minute.
