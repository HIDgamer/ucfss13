/datum/xeno_ai_controller/larva

Larva AI - "larva needs to hide, and just in general be scared since they are fragile." Unlike every other caste's controller, spotting a living hostile at all is the flee trigger here, not taking damage - a larva has no attack worth using and dies in one or two hits, so the generic approach-and-attack default (inherited from the base controller before this file existed) was actively suicidal. should_flee() is completely overridden rather than just tuning get_flee_threshold() higher, since the base version's health/ally-count/target-condition reasoning ("finish it off," "not fighting alone") all assume a xeno that can actually win a fight - none of that applies to her. She never enters AI_STATE_ATTACKING at all: the base tick()'s flee-check runs before movement/attack logic every tick, so an unconditional should_flee() intercepts her before she'd ever close distance on a target.
