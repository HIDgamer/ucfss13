/datum/xeno_ai_controller/praetorian

Praetorian AI - a melee-primary hybrid "warleader": closes with Dash like Crusher/Ravager's own dashes, throws Acid Ball as a heavy periodic hit in between plain swings, then deliberately breaks off - "hop into action, use its abilities, slash marines, and hop back into safety, and repeat." Per the user's explicit design ("use retreating in combat instead of low health = retreat"), this is a proactive hit-and-run rotation, not should_flee()'s health-threshold panic button - see the shared start_tactical_retreat()/is_tactical_retreating() helpers.
